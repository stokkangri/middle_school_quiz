1 · Drive (forward / back)

All four wheels spin the same way on each side. Front and back on one side match — the robot rolls forward or back.

All six wheels: left three share one power, right three share the other. Same power both sides → roll forward or back (tank / goBILDA starter style).

FL = drive · BL = drive · FR = drive · BR = drive

LEFT = drive · RIGHT = drive

2 · Turn (spin)

Left side goes one way, right side the other — the robot rotates in place.

FL = +turn · BL = +turn · FR = −turn · BR = −turn

LEFT = +turn · RIGHT = −turn

3 · Strafe (slide sideways)

On a normal robot, sideways is impossible. Mecanum rollers are angled (~45°). For strafe, front and back on the same side spin opposite ways.

  • Forward/back forces from those wheels cancel.
  • The angled rollers add up sideways — the robot slides left or right.

FL = +strafe · BL = −strafe · FR = −strafe · BR = +strafe

Try holding A or D and watch FL vs BL go opposite.

Strafe does not work on the 6-wheel goBILDA-style chassis — plain wheels have no angled rollers. Pressing A / D does nothing to the robot’s position.

To reach a spot beside you: turn 90° → drive → turn back. Use Demo: sideways goal to watch that path.

4 · TeleOp mix

FL = drive + strafe + turn
BL = drive − strafe + turn
FR = drive − strafe − turn
BR = drive + strafe − turn
LEFT  = drive + turn
RIGHT = drive − turn
(strafe is ignored)

5 · Different absolute speeds

Yes — left and right can spin at different |power|. Drive + turn (curve) is the clearest case — like a car taking a corner.

Press W+E or Demo curve ≠ speeds. Watch orange bars differ left vs right.

6 · Same place, no strafe

Goal is beside the robot. Mecanum: one strafe. 6-wheel: turn to face the goal → drive forward → turn to face front again. Same ending position; longer path without strafe.

Switch chassis and press Demo: sideways goal on each.

7 · Roller pattern (X · O · flipped)

Code can be perfect and strafe still fail if rollers are mounted wrong. Use Roller pattern above the keys, then hold A/D:

  • X — correct; side forces add → clean strafe.
  • O — common mistake; side forces cancel → almost no slide.
  • FR flipped — one wheel wrong → weak slide + unwanted spin/crab.

Watch the faint X/O on the chassis and the striped rollers on each wheel, then try Demo strafe.
