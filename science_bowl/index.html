Science Bowl Practice

Middle school · National Science Bowl sample questions

Questions come from the Department of Energy middle school sample sets for 2019 through 2022. DOE says to use them as sample questions, because some answers change as science advances.

When should the correct answer be shown?

Skip leaves a question blank. You can go back and answer it before you finish. Reveal shows the answer, and that question scores 0. Multiple choice stays in official W, X, Y, Z order.

Add questions for this practice

Paste one question object, an array of questions, or a bank that has a questions array. They join this practice until you refresh. To keep them, download the bank and replace questions.json.
